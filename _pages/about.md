I am a researcher in the field of machine learning and artificial intelligence. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), based in [Torr Vision Group](https://torrvision.com/index.html), working with Prof. [Philip Torr](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kPxa2w0AAAAJ&hl=en). Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) working with Prof. [Ricardo Silva](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=I-ANa0QAAAAJ&hl=en) from 2023 to 2025. I received my PhD degree in Computer Science from [Durham University](https://www.durham.ac.uk/) in 2023, advised by Prof. [Alexandra Cristea](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=jQMxCKYAAAAJ&hl=en). I have been actively serving as a member of the program committees in prestigious AI/ML venues (such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, IJCAI, AISTATS, UAI and KDD). I co-organized workshop on [Scaling Up Intervention Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/39963) at ICML 2025 and [Scaling Environments for Agents](https://nips.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/109540) at NeurIPS 2025.
