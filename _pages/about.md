I am a researcher in machine learning and artificial intelligence, with current interests in **causality, foundation models, and agents**. I am a postdoc at [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/) in [Torr Vision Group](https://torrvision.com/index.html), working with Prof. [Philip Torr](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kPxa2w0AAAAJ&hl=en). I am also a Junior Research Fellow (Non-stipendiary) in Computer Science at [Christ Church](https://www.chch.ox.ac.uk/people/dr-jialin-yu). Previously, I was a postdoc at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) with Prof. [Ricardo Silva](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=I-ANa0QAAAAJ&hl=en) from 2023 to 2025. I received my PhD degree in Computer Science from [Durham University](https://www.durham.ac.uk/) in 2023, advised by Prof. [Alexandra Cristea](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=jQMxCKYAAAAJ&hl=en). I co-organized workshops on [Scaling Up Intervention Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/39963) at ICML 2025 and [Scaling Environments for Agents](https://nips.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/109540) at NeurIPS 2025.

I am very open to research collaboration and happy to advise on external projects. Feel free to email me at `{firstname}.{lastname}@eng.ox.ac.uk`.
