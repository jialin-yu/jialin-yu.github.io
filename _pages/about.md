I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.bath.ac.uk/departments/department-of-computer-science/) at the University of Bath. I am also a Visiting Fellow at [TVG](https://torrvision.com/index.html) and a Stipendiary Lecturer at [St Catherine's College](https://www.stcatz.ox.ac.uk/), University of Oxford.

Previously, I was a postdoc at Oxford with Prof. [Philip Torr](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kPxa2w0AAAAJ&hl=en) and at [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) with Prof. [Ricardo Silva](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=I-ANa0QAAAAJ&hl=en). I was also a non-stipendiary Junior Research Fellow at [Christ Church](https://www.chch.ox.ac.uk/people/dr-jialin-yu) and earned my PhD at [Durham](https://www.durham.ac.uk/) under Prof. [Alexandra Cristea](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=jQMxCKYAAAAJ&hl=en). I co-organized workshops on [Scaling Up Intervention Models](https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/39963) at ICML 2025 and [Scaling Environments for Agents](https://nips.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/109540) at NeurIPS 2025.
