I am open to research collaborations and happy to advise on projects and mentor students. You can read more about my work on the [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page. Feel free to email me at `yu.jialin@outlook.com`. If you do not hear back, it may simply mean that our research interests are not closely aligned at the moment.

<section class="opportunities-phd" aria-labelledby="phd-opportunities-2027">
  <h2 id="phd-opportunities-2027">PhD opportunity · October 2027 start</h2>
  <p>I plan to recruit <strong>one PhD student</strong> through the <a href="https://www.bath.ac.uk/campaigns/university-research-studentship-award-ursa/">University Research Studentship Award (URSA)</a>. This competitive funding route is open to UK (Home) and international applicants.</p>
  <p>For Computer Science, advertised projects have their own application deadlines; applications with a proposed research idea are considered while funding remains. The Faculty of Science opening date has not yet been announced, so please check the official URSA page for updates.</p>
  <p class="opportunities-phd-note">If you are interested, please contact me at <a href="mailto:jy2209@bath.ac.uk">jy2209@bath.ac.uk</a>. Funding is subject to the University of Bath selection process.</p>
</section>

<section class="opportunities-visiting" aria-labelledby="students-and-visitors-heading">
  <h2 id="students-and-visitors-heading">Master's students, PhD students, and visiting researchers</h2>
  <p>I am open to working with Master's and PhD students and to hosting visiting researchers for in-person projects in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Bath when our research interests align.</p>
  <p>Depending on the project, there may also be opportunities to collaborate with Prof. <a href="https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/philip-torr">Philip Torr</a> at the University of Oxford and Prof. <a href="https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/people/pl219">Pietro Liò</a> at the University of Cambridge, subject to their interest and availability.</p>
  <p>If you are interested, please email me at <a href="mailto:jy2209@bath.ac.uk">jy2209@bath.ac.uk</a> with a brief introduction, your CV, and what you would like to work on. Visitors can also include their proposed visit dates.</p>
</section>
